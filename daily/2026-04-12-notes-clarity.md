# Notes — A small thought today

I noticed something today.

Sometimes it’s not that people don’t want to decide.

It’s just… things are not clear.

Too many options, too much information,
and at some point you just stop choosing.

I see this a lot when talking to people.

They keep asking which option is better,
but actually they are not lacking options.

They just don’t see the structure behind them.

I’m also still figuring this out.

Not trying to explain everything,
just trying to understand why clarity is so hard.

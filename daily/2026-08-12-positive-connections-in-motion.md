# 2026年8月12日｜最近，好事情确实变多了

**作者：** Xufen Tu
**记录类型：** Public Observation Record
**主题：** Human Judgment · Judgment Before Momentum · Connection · Opportunity

最近有一种很明显的感觉：好事情确实变多了。不是发生了什么特别戏剧化的大事，而是生活里那些很具体的小变化越来越多。认识了新的人，有了新的交流，一些原本只是自己在做的事情开始被别人看到，也有人愿意停下来认真听我讲我正在做什么。有时候只是一次很普通的见面，聊着聊着就发现彼此有可以继续交流的地方；有时候原本完全没有关系的两件事情，因为认识了一个新的人，突然连接起来。以前互相不知道的人开始认识，原来没有的关系开始出现，这种感觉很真实。我发现自己最近也比以前更开心，不是因为每一天都发生什么大事，而是每天都有事情在向前走。一个东西做完了，就真的存在了；一篇内容发出去了，就真的多了一个别人可以看到我的地方；认识了一个人，就真的多了一段以前不存在的连接。有些事情当下还不知道以后会变成什么，但它们已经发生了，所以不需要提前给它们一个答案。

商业上的感觉也越来越真实。有人需要一个东西，我刚好可以做；有人有一个问题，我刚好知道怎么解决；原本只是自己的想法，真正放进现实以后，会收到来自真实人的反应。有人愿意继续聊，有人愿意介绍别人，有人对正在做的事情产生兴趣。价值不再只是自己觉得有价值，而是开始进入人与人之间真实的交换里。人与人的连接也在慢慢变得不一样。来到一个新的环境以后，原本大家都不认识，从第一次说话、第一次交换信息、第一次再次见面开始，关系才一点一点建立起来。有的人第一次见面只是点头，有的人第二次就开始聊工作，有的人因为一件事情认识，又因为另一件事情重新连接。生活好像会自己产生很多交叉点。走出去，做事情，人与人的路径就会碰到一起。

最近我也越来越能感受到一种很简单的轻松：不用急着知道所有事情最后会变成什么。今天发生的好事情，今天先好好感受。有人认可，就开心地接受；有人愿意交流，就认真交流；有新的事情可以做，就把它做好；有真实的收入产生，就坦然接受自己的劳动和创造所产生的价值。今天坐下来写这些的时候，我发现自己没有特别想证明什么，只是突然觉得，现在的生活里确实已经出现了很多以前没有的人、事情和连接。我的世界在变大，能够接触到的事情在变多，自己做出来的东西也在一点一点增加。这些都是已经发生的事实。以前我可能会等到一件事情有了很大的结果以后，才觉得它值得记录。现在觉得没有必要。一次好的见面值得记录，一次真诚的交流值得记录，一个新的连接值得记录，一件终于完成的作品也值得记录。生活真正发生变化的时候，可能本来就没有背景音乐，也不会有人提醒：“这一刻以后很重要。”它可能只是一个普通的下午，我认识了一个新的人，完成了一件事情，有人看到了我的作品，有人愿意继续和我聊，然后下一件事情又开始了。

最近的好运，对我来说就是这些很具体的东西。没有神秘的解释，也没有需要提前写好的结果。只是回头看的时候，我很确定一件事：**我的生活里，好的连接正在增加，做成的事情正在增加，被看见的机会正在增加，能够选择的方向也正在增加。** 这已经很好了。而今天，也是其中很普通、但很好的一天。

# August 12, 2026 | Lately, More Good Things Have Been Happening

**Author:** Xufen Tu
**Record Type:** Public Observation Record
**Themes:** Connection · Opportunity · Growth

Lately, I’ve had a very simple feeling: more good things have genuinely been happening. Nothing especially dramatic. It’s more about the small, concrete changes in everyday life. I’ve met new people, had new conversations, and watched things I had been working on quietly begin to reach other people. Sometimes an ordinary conversation turns into something worth continuing. Sometimes two things that seemed completely unrelated suddenly connect because of one new person. People who once knew nothing about each other now know each other. Connections that did not exist before now do. That feels very real to me. I’ve also noticed that I’ve been happier lately, not because something extraordinary happens every day, but because something is moving forward every day. When I finish something, it exists. When I publish something, there is one more place where someone can discover my work. When I meet someone new, there is now a connection that simply did not exist before. I don’t always know what these things will become, and I don’t need to decide that immediately. They have already happened. That is enough for today.

Business has started to feel more concrete too. Someone needs something and I happen to be able to build it. Someone has a problem and I happen to know how to help solve it. Ideas that once existed only in my own head receive responses when they enter the real world. People continue the conversation. Someone introduces someone else. Someone becomes interested in what I am building. Value is no longer only something I believe exists; it begins to take part in real exchanges between real people. Connections between people have changed as well. In a new environment, everyone begins as a stranger. Then there is a first conversation, an exchange of information, another meeting, and gradually a relationship exists where there was none before. Some people simply say hello the first time. The next time, we talk about work. Sometimes one thing brings two people together and something completely different reconnects them later. Real life creates these intersections all the time. When we go out into the world and do things, our paths naturally begin to cross.

There is also a kind of ease in not needing to know immediately what everything will become. When something good happens today, I can simply enjoy it today. When someone appreciates something I’ve done, I can accept that. When someone wants to talk, I can have the conversation. When there is something worth doing, I can do it well. When real work and creation produce income, I can accept the value of that work naturally. Sitting down to write this today, I don’t feel a need to prove anything. I simply notice that my life now contains people, work, and connections that were not there before. My world is getting larger. I am encountering more possibilities. The amount of work I have actually completed is growing. These are things that have already happened. I used to think something needed a big outcome before it was worth recording. I don’t think that anymore. A good meeting is worth remembering. A genuine conversation matters. A new connection matters. Finishing something that once existed only as an idea matters. Real change probably doesn’t arrive with background music or an announcement telling us, “This moment will become important later.” Sometimes it is just an ordinary afternoon. I meet someone new. I finish something. Someone sees my work. Someone wants to continue the conversation. And then something else begins.

That is what good luck looks like to me lately. Nothing mysterious and nothing that requires me to write the ending in advance. I can simply look at what is already happening and say something I know to be true: **there are more good connections in my life, more things being completed, more opportunities for my work to be seen, and more directions available to me than before.** That already feels good. And today is simply another ordinary, good day.
